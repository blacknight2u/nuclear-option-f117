# F-117A Nighthawk v0.4.88

Initial public release of the F-117A Nighthawk for Nuclear Option.

## Highlights

- Separate rank 4, $120M stealth strike aircraft; no stock aircraft replacement
- Dynamic radar signature that responds progressively to both weapon bays and the landing gear
- Twin non-afterburning engines, passive targeting, and game-native cockpit displays
- Fixed burst-limited game-native jammer, 64 chaff, and 32 flares
- Host-authoritative requisition inventory and rank-4 factory production in normal missions
- Automatic delegation to Equalizer when it is active, preventing duplicate production
- Expanded internal precision-strike, anti-radar, anti-ship, and defensive air-to-air loadouts
- Animated landing gear, bomb bays, canopy, and landing drag chute
- Sectioned native damage model with detachable control surfaces and visible battle damage
- Nighthawk Black and Farewell Flag liveries
- Multiplayer support when all players use matching game, Blueprinter, and F-117 versions

Requires Nuclear Option 0.34.2 and Blueprinter 1.8.21.
